Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 3, 2026

At Khaima al-Riyada Gym (Marol Branch), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information through the FitTracker system:

  • Personal Identity: Name, TR number, ITS number, and date of birth.
  • Fitness Data: Weight, height, body measurements, and fitness test results.
  • Activity Logs: Attendance records, workout plans, and performance data.
  • Health Information: Medical history and disclosure of injuries (for safety purposes).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Managing gym enrollment and slot assignments.
  • Tracking your fitness progress and providing personalized feedback.
  • Calculating gamification rewards (XP, levels, and badges).
  • Ensuring safety during physical exercise.
  • Administrative reporting within Al Jamea Tus Saifiyah.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored on secure servers with encrypted password protection. We use industry-standard security measures to prevent unauthorized access or data breaches.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or lease your personal data to third parties. Access to your information is limited to authorized staff, trainers, and evaluators within the Khaima al-Riyada management team.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to view your recorded data through your FitTracker student dashboard. For any corrections or data deletion requests (upon leaving the institute), please contact the admin team.

6. Cookies

The FitTracker system uses session cookies to maintain your login state. These cookies do not track your activity on other websites.
